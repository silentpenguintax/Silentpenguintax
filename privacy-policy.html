1. Introduction

Silent Penguin PLLC ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services, including our online tax preparation portal.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address
  • Financial Information: Income, deductions, investment details, business information necessary for tax preparation
  • Tax Documents: W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, brokerage statements, and other tax-related documents
  • Identification: Social Security Number, Employer Identification Number (EIN), and other identifying information required for tax filing
  • Communication: Any information you include when you contact us via email, phone, or our contact forms

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device type
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, links clicked, referral source
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies: We use cookies to enhance user experience and analyze website usage

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining our tax preparation and accounting services
  • Preparing and filing your tax returns and other tax documents
  • Communicating with you about your account and services
  • Responding to your inquiries and providing customer support
  • Sending service updates, newsletters, and promotional communications (with your consent)
  • Improving our website, services, and user experience
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Fraud detection and prevention
  • Meeting professional tax and accounting standards

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your information based on the following legal grounds:

  • Contract Performance: Processing is necessary to provide the services you've requested
  • Legal Obligation: Processing is required by tax law, accounting standards, and regulatory requirements
  • Legitimate Interest: Processing is necessary for our business operations and to prevent fraud
  • Consent: You have explicitly consented to certain types of processing

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. However, we may share your information with:

  • Tax Authorities: IRS, state tax agencies, and other government entities as required by law
  • Financial Institutions: Banks and financial platforms to facilitate tax compliance
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting providers, document management systems, and email platforms that help us deliver services (all bound by confidentiality agreements)
  • Sub-contractors: CPA professionals and tax specialists who assist with your tax preparation under confidentiality obligations
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law or court order
  • Professional Standards: As required by AICPA, IRS Circular 230, or other professional regulatory bodies

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/SSL)
  • Encrypted data storage
  • Secure client portal with password protection and two-factor authentication
  • Limited employee access with role-based permissions
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Confidentiality agreements with all staff and contractors

Note: While we implement robust security measures, no transmission of data over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations. Generally, we maintain tax records for:

  • The current tax year plus 7 years (IRS standard retention period)
  • Longer periods if required by law or for ongoing matters

You may request deletion of certain non-essential information at any time, provided it does not conflict with legal retention requirements.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from promotional communications
  • Portability: Request your information in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@silentpenguintax.com.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites before sharing your information.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). For information about your California privacy rights, contact us directly.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
