Services

Our Big 4 Core Services

Silent Penguin Tax Services provides modern, fully remote CPA tax services for individuals and small businesses across the United States. Our firm focuses on efficient, secure, and professional tax solutions designed for today's digital environment.

Core Service 01 Individual Tax Services Best for: W-2 earners, investors, and multi-state filers

We provide comprehensive federal and state income tax preparation for individuals with a wide range of financial situations. Our streamlined remote process allows clients to complete their tax filing securely without in-person meetings.

Common Situations We Handle

  • W-2 employees and professionals
  • Investment income and capital gains
  • Stock compensation (RSU, ESPP, stock options)
  • Multi-state tax filings
  • Rental property income and expenses
  • High-income taxpayers
  • Prior year amendments and corrections

What's Included

  • CPA preparation and review
  • Secure document upload portal
  • Electronic filing (IRS and state)
  • Post-filing support
Core Service 02 Small Business Tax Services Best for: entrepreneurs and owner-operated businesses

We work with entrepreneurs, independent professionals, and small business owners to ensure accurate tax compliance while supporting long-term growth.

Entity Types Supported

  • Sole Proprietors (Schedule C)
  • Partnerships (Form 1065)
  • S Corporations (Form 1120-S)

Services Include

  • Business tax return preparation
  • Owner and business tax coordination
  • Estimated tax calculation
  • Reasonable compensation guidance
  • Entity structure consultation
  • Ongoing tax compliance support
Core Service 03 Tax Planning & Advisory Best for: proactive strategy and year-round tax decisions

Tax planning goes beyond filing returns. We provide proactive planning strategies to help clients make informed financial decisions throughout the year.

Planning Services

  • Estimated tax planning

  • Entity selection analysis
  • Retirement contribution planning
  • Income timing strategies
  • Multi-state tax considerations

Tax planning engagements are tailored based on each client's financial situation and objectives.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our process, pricing, and service.

Do you meet clients in person?

No. We're a fully remote firm. Everything happens through our secure online portal and email. This is by design and keeps the process efficient.

Which states do you serve?

All 50 states. We prepare individual and business returns nationwide, including multi-state filings.

How do I upload documents?

Once you pay, we set up your secure portal access. You can then upload W-2s, 1099s, and supporting records directly.

What's the typical turnaround time?

Most individual returns are completed in 7-10 business days after final documents are submitted. Business timelines vary by complexity.

When do I pay?

After you receive and accept your quote, payment is completed online before work begins.

Can you amend prior year returns?

Yes. We prepare amended returns (Form 1040-X) when prior filings need correction.

Do you work year-round?

Yes. We operate year-round for filing, planning, amendments, and business tax support.

What documents do I need?

At minimum, W-2s, 1099s, and income records. We send a full checklist after intake based on your situation.
