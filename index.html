With over 10 years of professional tax preparation and consulting experience, I established Silent Penguin to provide a modern alternative to traditional accounting firms - combining professional tax expertise with secure, technology-driven efficiency. My goal is simple: deliver clear tax strategy, accurate compliance, and a streamlined client experience without unnecessary complexity.

Silent Penguin PLLC is built on precision, transparency, and responsiveness - ensuring clients can move forward with clarity and confidence.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our process, pricing, and service.

Do you meet clients in person?

No. We're a fully remote firm. Everything happens through our secure online portal and email. This is by design and keeps the process efficient.

Which states do you serve?

All 50 states. We prepare individual and business returns nationwide, including multi-state filings.

How do I upload documents?

Once you pay, we set up your secure portal access. You can then upload W-2s, 1099s, and supporting records directly.

What's the typical turnaround time?

Most individual returns are completed in 7-10 business days after final documents are submitted. Business timelines vary by complexity.

When do I pay?

After you receive and accept your quote, payment is completed online before work begins.

Can you amend prior year returns?

Yes. We prepare amended returns (Form 1040-X) when prior filings need correction.

Do you work year-round?

Yes. We operate year-round for filing, planning, amendments, and business tax support.

What documents do I need?

At minimum, W-2s, 1099s, and income records. We send a full checklist after intake based on your situation.
